Why it cannot select an intervention
------------------------------------
The 0.175 rad band is not a sag detector for a bang-bang walking policy. On this run's
OWN nominal walking telemetry, with no degradation applied at all, per-joint p99 of
|requested - q| reaches 0.69 rad and 9 of 144 joint-sessions sustain the latch condition
for 0.5 s or more (one for 10.18 s). Nominal never latches only because the latch is
armed exclusively when a degradation spec is present; a no-op scale-1.0 spec would latch
a healthy robot. In the leg_RR 0.80 cell the trip count was 6 of 144, NO HIGHER than
nominal's 9 of 144.

So the measured "degradation" is substantially the fraction of episodes tripping a latch
sized for a different task, not graded locomotion degradation. Splitting leg_RR at 0.80:

  latch fired       90/384 episodes   walking success 0.000   min base height 0.106 m
  latch did not     294/384 episodes  walking success 0.956   min base height 0.281 m
  nominal reference 384 episodes      walking success 0.922   min base height 0.282 m

Three quarters of episodes walk as well as nominal; the rest are stopped by the gate.
Only 1.4 % of un-held degraded episodes ever breach the 0.20 m height bound.

What it shows
-------------
Every C2/C3/C4 cell fails the preregistered not_catastrophic rule, on safety-hold rates
of 0.206 to 0.841 and fidelity of 0.79 down to 0.16. Dose-response is monotone in all
four families (worst reversal +0.0000). C1 (single joint) fails on magnitude regardless
of the latch: max pooled drop 0.0495 at s = 0.50 against the 0.15 bar, which
independently reproduces amendment 12 on fresh seeds with the more sensitive endpoint.
